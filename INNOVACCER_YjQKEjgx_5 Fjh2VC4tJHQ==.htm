About the Role
We are building a world-class Enterprise Growth organization designed for individuals who want to operate at the intersection of strategy, problem-solving, and high-stakes enterprise sales. As an Intern-Strategic Business Development, you will be the front line of market engagement, diagnosing challenges for CXOs, shaping problem statements, creating demand, and architecting the path toward large-scale transformation pilots.

A Day in the Life
** Own the complete lead generation & management process from lead capture to follow-up and tracking them through the entire sales cycle
** Responsible for driving outbound prospecting through cold calls, personalized email campaigns, and targeted LinkedIn outreach to generate qualified pipeline.
** Coordinate with the prospects and the frontend team to set up business meetings/calls. Further, briefing the frontend team about the prospect after conducting secondary research and collating relevant information.
** Engage in dialogue to understand client requirements and expectations
** Work collaboratively with Sales Team and obtain feedback on the quality of MQLs generated from marketing campaigns
** Track full-funnel lead metrics and provide recommendations: program and campaign investments based on conversion rates.
** Measure, analyze, and report campaign performance and deliver weekly campaign performance briefs to leadership
** Drive data capture and management. Provide regular database health checks, cleansing, and augmentation
** Partner closely with product marketing and campaign management teams to ensure proper messaging, timing and execution of one-off and ongoing campaigns across email and web personalization
** Use the latest in account-based marketing technology to run multi-channel campaigns targeting Innovaccer’s largest customers
** Knowledge of Sales funnel and pipeline structure
** Should be comfortable working in the US shift timings of 5:30 PM to 2 AM.

What You Need
** Analytical Mindset: Comfortable with Excel/Google Sheets and eager to learn data-driven analysis and reporting. Strong analytical skills with marketing science
** Strong Organizational Skills: Able to juggle multiple tasks and maintain attention to detail in a fast-paced environment.
** Communication & Collaboration: Willing to learn from experienced professionals, proactively ask questions, and share ideas. Good written and verbal communication skills for reporting campaign progress and results.
** Passion & Curiosity: Eagerness to dive into marketing operations, explore new tools, and keep up with industry best practices.
