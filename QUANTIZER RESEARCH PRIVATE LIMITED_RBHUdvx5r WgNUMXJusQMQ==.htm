Job Profile Details:

About the Role
As a QA Engineer in the High-Frequency Trading domain, you will be responsible for testing and ensuring the robustness and performance of HFT systems. You will work closely with quantitative traders, developers, and others to validate and ensure the functionality, scalability, and low-latency performance of the systems. This position offers the opportunity to work on complex, high-performance trading algorithms and platforms in a fast-paced environment.

What will you do
** Develop and execute comprehensive test plans and test cases to validate HFT systems and applications
** Work closely with developers and quantitative analysts to ensure trading algorithms are correctly implemented and robust.
** Monitor, validate and test the unification of trading systems with various market exchanges and liquidity providers.
** Perform functional, performance, scalability, and integration testing of trading platforms and associated components.
** Document and report defects, working with development teams to resolve issues swiftly and accurately.
** Automate test cases where applicable, especially for performance and regression testing.

What will you bring
** Ability to analyze system performance and troubleshoot issues in a complex, multi-system environment.
** Knowledge of networking protocols (TCP/IP, UDP) and experience with distributed systems.
** Excellent problem-solving and analytical skills to identify potential issues and resolve them quickly.
** Ability to work in a fast-paced and collaborative environment, with a keen eye for detail.
** Ability to write clear and concise documentation.
** Proficiency in at least in one of the programming languages such as C++ or python.

Perks & Benefits
** Exposure to cutting-edge technologies and innovative systems in the financial trading industry.
** Opportunities to collaborate with experienced professionals in trading, quantitative analysis, and software development.
** Competitive compensation and excellent bonus programs
** Comprehensive health insurance coverage for employees and their families
** Annual Extravaganza
