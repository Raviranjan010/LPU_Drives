Role: Intern - Business Analyst

Your Mission:
As a Business Analyst, you will be at the heart of innovation, engineering intelligence into real-world solutions that shape the future of businesses across industries.
** A Natural Translator of Business to Tech: You can listen to a client’s complex business problem and seamlessly translate it into clear, actionable technical requirements. You connect business context with data and delivery.
** A Precision-Focused Organizer: You thrive in structured chaos—drafting BRDs, MoMs, and weekly updates comes naturally to you. You ensure no insight or action point is lost between meetings, sprints, or reports.
** A Strategic Collaborator: Whether working with architects to shape solutions or aligning with delivery leads to assign resources, you operate at the intersection of teams—making sure each one is moving toward the same outcome.
** A Confident Communicator: From leading sprint demos to interacting with global clients across onsite and offshore projects, you’re clear, persuasive, and always aligned with the “why” behind the “what.”
** A Curious Analyst: You don’t stop at the obvious—you explore raw data to spot trends, anomalies, and opportunities, and you translate those into insights that truly move the business forward.
** A Long-Term Thinker with a Bias for Action: You’re not only focused on solving the problem today—you can envision the long-term impact of the solution and ensure it’s executed with excellence.

Role: Intern- Software Developer

Your Mission:
As a Software Developer, you will be at the heart of innovation, engineering intelligence into real-world solutions that shape the future of businesses across industries.
** A Builder’s Mindset: You love turning ideas into working code. Whether it’s building APIs, crafting intuitive UIs, or designing full-stack applications, you take pride in building things that work well. Also to build reusable code and libraries for future use.
** A Passion for Performance: You don’t just write code, you optimize it. Speed, scalability, and security are not afterthoughts—they’re part of your design from day one.
** A Structured, Logical Approach: You can take a complex problem, break it down, and architect smart, scalable solutions. Your database schemas are clean, efficient, and future-proof.
** An Eye for Design: Whether you're building server-side portals or user-facing dashboards, you know that clean, functional design makes all the difference.
** A Spirit of Collaboration: You enjoy working alongside teammates and stakeholders, turning feedback into features and building better products together.

Role: Intern- Machine Learning Engineer

Your Mission:
As a Machine Learning Engineer, you will be at the heart of innovation, engineering intelligence into real-world solutions that shape the future of businesses across industries.
** Engineer Intelligence: Harness the power of both structured and unstructured data to design, build, and train machine learning models using traditional algorithms and cutting-edge deep learning techniques.
** Collaborate to Create Impact: Work hand-in-hand with solution architects and cross-functional teams to engineer tailored AI solutions that directly address our clients toughest challenges.
** Own the Pipeline: From data preprocessing to model deployment, take full ownership of the end-to-end ML lifecycle, ensuring scalable, production-ready solutions on leading cloud platforms.
** Explore New Frontiers: Dive into a spectrum of domains - Be it’s decoding images through computer vision, translating human intent in natural language processing, or transforming audio in speech recognition. Your work will touch the core of how machines understand the world.
** Scale with the Cloud: Leverage the scale & power of platforms like AWS, Azure, and GCP to build robust, cloud-native AI solutions that perform at enterprise scale and speed.

Role: Intern- Business Analyst Sales Engineer

Your Mission:
As a Business Analyst Sales Engineer, you will be on the front lines, working with a highly energetic team to shape the future of our clients.
** Become a Strategist: Partner with our technical and sales experts to map the competitive landscape for our clients, identifying their most pressing needs and charting the course for how Quantiphi's solutions can lead their transformation.
** Excel at Solution Definition: Deconstruct complex business challenges, methodically capture requirements, and help scope the innovative Data, AI solutions, leveraging the power of Cloud that form the foundation of our client proposals.
** Architect a Clear Vision: Craft and deliver compelling presentations that outline a clear vision for a client's technological transformation, demonstrating how our solutions can reshape their entire business.
** Build the Business Case: Weave powerful narratives using Quantiphi's incredible success stories to build trust and prove why we are the world's leading AI-first digital engineering partner.

Role: Intern- Platform Engineer

Your Mission:
As a Platform Engineer, you will be at the heart of innovation, engineering intelligence into real-world solutions that shape the future of businesses across industries.
** A Builder’s Mindset: You enjoy tinkering with systems, writing scripts, finding smarter and faster ways to get things done. You take pride in building foundations.
** A Bias for Simplicity: You believe in writing clean, efficient code—even for the smallest scripts. You aim to automate the boring stuff, so your team can focus on what truly matters.
** An Eye for Stability: You’re excited by the challenge of making systems not just work—but scale, self-heal, and perform at their best, even under pressure.
** A Collaborative Heart: You love working alongside software engineers, data scientists, and ML teams—learning from them, supporting them, and shipping code that makes everyone’s work smoother.
** An Insatiable Curiosity: You’re always picking up new tools, experimenting with emerging technologies, and exploring what’s next in cloud, DevOps, and platform engineering.
** A Commitment to Clarity: Whether it’s writing a script, debugging a service, or documenting your work, you make it easy for others to follow and build on your efforts.

Role: Intern- Data Engineer

Your Mission:
As a Data Engineer, you will be at the heart of innovation, engineering intelligence into real-world solutions that shape the future of businesses across industries.
** A Passion for Data Craftsmanship: You don’t just write pipelines—you build intelligent, scalable systems that bring raw data to life. You're excited by the idea of shaping how data flows across an organization.
** A Builder’s Mindset: Whether it's designing robust Fact and Dimension tables or developing complex SQL logic, you approach problems with a clear architecture and a hunger to improve efficiency and reliability.
** An Eye for Quality: You dive deep into data profiling, understand inconsistencies, and transform chaos into clarity. You take pride in clean, validated, well-documented pipelines.
** A Problem-Solving Instinct: You see messy data as a puzzle. Whether it’s debugging a broken ETL job or designing test cases, you’re quick to isolate issues and persistent in fixing them.
** A Communicator of Insights: You understand the power of visualizing data to influence decisions. Your work enables business users to make smarter, faster choices.
