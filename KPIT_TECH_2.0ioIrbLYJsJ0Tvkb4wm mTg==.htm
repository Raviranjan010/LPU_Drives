Job Profile Details:

KPIT Technologies is offering an exciting opportunity for B.Tech students from NOVA colleges graduating in 2027, 2028, & 2029 to showcase their skills through a project-based contest. This initiative provides a direct path to a Pre-Placement Offer (PPO) with KPIT.

Contest Overview: The contest is open to B.Tech students from 2027, 2028, and 2029 graduating batches will be conducted in two phases.

Theme:
** AI Systems
** Energy
** Mobility (Drones)
** Simulation
** Wearable Robotics

Problem Statement
** Decentralized Multi-Agent Coordination for Autonomous Logistics

Phase 1: Idea Submission

1. Team Composition: 4 students + 1 mentor
** Teams can have up to 4 with at least 50% female diversity.
** Individual participation is allowed.
** KPIT Certified faculty can act as mentors for student participants.

2. Submission Process:
** Students will submit their ideas to the KPIT-trained college faculty for initial evaluation.
** The college will filter the submissions, and the top 3 ideas will be nominated by the college faculty.
** The idea should be relevant to the automotive industry and KPIT’s core business areas, demonstrating technical proficiency and innovation.
** Out of the 3 teams the best idea will be submitted to KPIT for further evaluation.

3. Evaluation Criteria & Rating for 3 nominated teams:


Phase 2: Evaluation & Jury Presentation
** A shortlisted team will present their ideas in the form of PPT to a panel of KPIT experts and leaders via Microsoft Teams.
** The panel will assess projects based on technical depth, innovation, and impact.

Eligibility Criteria

** Participant Profile: Open to B. Tech students graduating in 2027, 2028, and 2029.
** Pre-requisites: Students must have 70% and above in their entire educational career

Prizes & Benefits-
** Prize: Goodies worth 5K
** PPO from KPIT
** Fast-track onboarding
** Fast-Track Genesis Batch: Winners will be placed in a specialized training program, ensuring accelerated training, incubation, and deployment into real-world projects.
** SPARKLE Advantage: Direct entry into the SPARKLE Finale, provided the idea aligns with that year’s theme.

This initiative is a unique opportunity for students to gain hands-on experience, demonstrate their technical expertise, and secure a career opportunity with KPIT.
