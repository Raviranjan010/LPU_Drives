Job Profile Details:

A campus-to-corporate connect, made real.
** Capgemini Brand Quest is your opportunity to get to know Capgemini up close. Explore our culture, values, people, technologies, and more in a fun & challenging way. Compete with talented students from across the country, test your knowledge, and unlock exciting rewards and unforgettable experiences along the way.
** This is your chance to unearth what makes Capgemini one of the world's leading transformation partners and a great place to work. The contest will determine one winner, two runners-up, and seven finalists, all 10 will receive rewards based on their rankings.

5 reasons to play:
** Winner walks away with a hospitality experience worth INR 1 Lakh*.
** Top 10 finalists will receive hospitality experience vouchers from a pool of INR ~2 Lakh.
** An all-expenses-covered trip to Capgemini's campus in Pune to the top 10 finalists and a chance to network with Capgemini colleagues.
** A few lucky draw winners will receive branded merchandise upon registration.
** The ‘Top 10 Most Engaged’ college gets a trophy.

Note: The total prize amount of INR 2 Lakhs will be distributed across various winner categories.

Eligibility
** The contest is open to students from selected colleges in India having an existing relationship with Capgemini. Participation in this event is through invitation only and voluntary. All eligible students will receive intimation from their respective college/university and/or Capgemini, inviting them to register and participate in the event. Any alternative methods of registration by students, such as usage of forwarded emails or messages from friends at other colleges, will be considered invalid. Registration does not grant the right to play in the quiz. The organizer is not liable to inform students whose registrations are considered invalid at our end. The set of eligible colleges is available but not displayed here to maintain privacy. A list of invited colleges can be produced only for legal, audit and other statutory compliance purposes on demand only to concerned authorities. Capgemini reserves the right to change the terms and conditions of the contest, including eligibility, without any further 
communication.

Quiz Mechanism
** All questions in the quiz will be about Capgemini and based on publicly available information, facts, and figures including the Capgemini's website (Group & India). Also, keep following the social media channels of Capgemini India page on Facebook, Instagram, and LinkedIn.

Registration
** All eligible students will receive link to register for the event. A confirmation mail from the organizer or the service provider appointed by Capgemini will confirm the participation of the student in the event. The registration period to participate in the Capgemini Brand Quest 2026 is till 31.08.2026. Any registration beyond this period will not be considered for participation purposes.

Level 1
** The Level 1 round will be conducted virtually and will be scheduled from 04.09.2026 to 05.09.2026. The Level 1 online quiz round will be conducted on third party quiz platform (Superset). The quiz will consist of 20 Multiple-Choice Questions (MCQs) carrying 10 marks each, with a total score of 200. The duration of the quiz will be 30 minutes. To ensure the best performance and user experience, the quiz is optimized for laptop and desktop devices. Mobile access is also supported, subject to adequate network connectivity and device performance.
** Note: For Level 1 evaluation, scores will be the sole criteria for qualification. In the Event of a tie, priority will be given to the participant who completes the quiz with the least amount of time. If a participant fails to complete the quiz within this 30-minute window, the quiz will be automatically submitted, and the quiz window will close, as the quiz is time-bound and automated. It is the participants' responsibility to manage their time effectively within the allocated slot. The top 20% students from each college will be selected basis their score and shortest time taken to complete the test. Organizers will also apply benchmarking time (i.e.-minimum amount of time taken to complete the test in ideal scenarios by the best-known experts. The benchmarking time will not be revealed). Candidates who score high at the shortest time taken will qualify (shortest time taken by candidate must be equal to or higher than the benchmarking time set by the organizer).

Level 2
** The Level 2 round will consist of two online quiz rounds (Part A & Part B) and will be conducted virtually on a third-party platform (Superset).

Level 2: Part A
** Scheduled on 29.09.2026 - 30.09.2026.
** The top 20% students from each college who qualify from Level 1 will participate in this round.
** The quiz will consist of 20 Multiple-Choice Questions (MCQs).
** Marks: 5 marks per question | Total Score: 100
** Duration: 30 minutes
** Based on scores and shortest time taken, the top 3,000 participants will qualify for Level 2: Part B.

Level 2: Part B
** Scheduled on 09.10.2026 - 10.10.2026.
** Only 3,000 qualified participants from Part A will be eligible to participate.
** The quiz will consist of 15 Multiple-Choice Questions (MCQs).
** Marks: 10 marks per question | Total Score: 150
** Duration: 15 minutes
** Based on scores and shortest time taken, the top 30 participants will qualify for Level 3 - Jury Round.

Note: For Level 2 rounds, evaluation will be based on scores and shortest time taken. In the event of a tie, preference will be given to the participant who completes the quiz in the least amount of time.
** If a participant fails to complete the quiz within the allotted duration, the quiz will be automatically submitted at the end of the scheduled time window. Participants are responsible for managing their time effectively during the assessment.
** Organizers may also apply a benchmarking time, which represents the minimum amount of time required to complete the assessment under ideal conditions. This benchmarking time will remain confidential. Participants must meet or exceed the benchmarking threshold while securing high scores to be considered for qualification.

Level 3 - Jury Round
** The Top 30 participants from Level 2 will be selected based on the total score and invited to participate in the Level 3 - Jury Round, scheduled between 19 October 2026 and 30 October 2026.

The Jury Round will consist of two parts:
Part A: Presentation Round
** Participants will deliver a presentation on a generic topic related to Innovation and Technology. This round will assess their knowledge, originality of thought, communication skills, and ability to articulate ideas effectively.

Part B: Personality & Brand Quotient Interview
** Participants will attend a personal interview with a jury panel comprising representatives from Capgemini India. The interview will assess participants' brand quotient, communication skills, leadership potential, overall personality, and alignment with Capgemini's values through personality-based and behavioral questions.
** This round will be evaluated based on participants' overall performance across both components. The jury panel from Capgemini shall have the sole discretion to evaluate participants and select the Top 10 finalists for the Grand Finale. Selection of the Top 10 finalists will be based on the combined scores obtained in Part A and Part B. The decision of the jury shall be final and binding.

Note:
** The Jury round will take place on the virtual platform and to be taken via laptop/desktop (preferably Microsoft Teams - online meeting tool), please ensure that both video and audio are enabled. Failure to enable either one may result in disqualification. One of the selection criteria for candidates qualifying for the Jury Round is punctuality. Coming on time for the interview is essential for consideration. The panel conducting the evaluation will remain undisclosed and will consist of Capgemini colleagues from various job levels and roles. The participants' personal interview video call will be recorded by the organizers for evaluation and auditing purposes. Non-compliance may lead to disqualification. All candidates are advised to bring their college ID card and Government ID proof for self-identification with the panel.
** Candidates must attend the Jury Round on the date and time slot assigned to them. As scheduling constraints may apply, the organizer may not be able to accommodate requests for alternate slots or provide another opportunity to participate in the Jury Round.

Grand Finale
** The top 10 participants will be selected to compete in the Grand Finale, which will take place at the Capgemini Office in Pune, India. The selection of participants for Grand Finale will be determined based on the final scores of Level 3. Other criteria for selection are social media profile screening, and participants' availability to appear for Grand Finale Event in Pune. The Grand Finale is planned for December 2026.
