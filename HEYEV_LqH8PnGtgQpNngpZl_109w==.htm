About the Role:
We are looking for a passionate and driven Full Stack Intern with strong experience in ReactJS, NodeJS (ExpressJS), and PostgreSQL to join our growing development team. You will work towards building and maintaining modern, scalable, and high-performance web applications. This is a fantastic opportunity for someone looking to apply their knowledge in a real-world environment and make a tangible impact in a fast-paced, product-driven team.

Key Responsibilities:
** Build, test, and deploy scalable web applications using ReactJS, NodeJS, and PostgreSQL
** Write clean, maintainable, and well-documented code
** Optimize applications for maximum performance and scalability
** Debug and troubleshoot issues across the stack
** Integrate RESTful APIs and third-party services
** Participate in code reviews, stand-ups, and agile ceremonies

Required Skills & Qualifications:
** Minimum 6 months of hands-on experience building full-stack web applications
** Proficiency with ReactJS, including hooks, state management (e.g., Context API, Redux), and component lifecycle
** Strong knowledge of NodeJS / ExpressJS for backend development
** Experience working with PostgreSQL, including writing complex queries and designing schemas
** Familiarity with RESTful API design and integration
** Solid understanding of Git and version control workflows
** Comfortable working with tools like Postman, Docker (optional), and basic CI/CD
** Ability to work independently and in a team environment
** Excellent problem-solving and communication skills
** Experience with cloud platforms like AWS is a plus

About Company:

HeyEV is building the operating layer for EV mobility in India.We believe the future of mobility is electric—but putting millions of EVs on the road requires much more than vehicles.It requires access to capital, reliable assets, intelligent technology, and on-ground service working together.HeyEV brings these layers together into one integrated platform—connecting EV assets, leasing & financing, IoT-driven intelligence, and service infrastructure.We work across the ecosystem with drivers, gig workers, fleet operators, OEMs, dealers, NBFCs and mobility platforms to make EV mobility more accessible, reliable and scalable.For drivers and gig workers, we make it easier to access productive EV assets and build better livelihoods.For fleet operators, we enable access to assets, financing, visibility and service without the complexity of managing fragmented providers.For OEMs and financial institutions, we provide the distribution, technology and operating infrastructure required to scale EV adoption.Today, our focus is on India’s last-mile and gig mobility economy, with a growing presence across cities and thousands of micro-entrepreneurs already supported.But our ambition goes much further.We want HeyEV to become the largest asset-access and leasing platform powering India’s gig economy—starting with EVs and expanding wherever productive assets can help people earn.Assets.
