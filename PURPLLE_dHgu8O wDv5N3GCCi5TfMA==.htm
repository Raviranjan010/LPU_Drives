Engineering Intern
THE PURPLLE WAY: AI-NATIVE ENGINEERING
We believe that as a business scales, technology shouldn’t become more fragile. We have redesigned our entire engineering lifecycle to be AI-native, moving away from manual toil toward a world of intelligent, self governing systems:
** Velocity without Fear (70% AI-Assisted): We ship fast because we’ve built the safety nets to do so. 
** You won’t just be a “coder”; you will be a system architect. We empower our engineers to use AI for the heavy lifting of development—aiming for 70% of Pull Requests to be AI-assisted—so you can focus on complex logic and high-level architecture.
** Built-in Guardrails & Self-Healing Systems: Security and quality aren’t “extra steps”—they are baked into our “Golden Paths.” We build systems that use AI for automated rollbacks and deterministic fallback logic, ensuring the platform absorbs mistakes automatically and remains resilient under pressure.
** Intelligence by Default (Zero-Toil Operations): We embed AI across the entire lifecycle—Build, Test, Detect, and Operate. By utilizing AI agents to eliminate “Ops Toil,” we’ve created an environment where anomaly detection and incident diagnosis happen in minutes, not hours.
** Predictable Economics & Growth: We treat cloud costs and system performance as core engineering disciplines. Every engineer at Purplle understands the “unit economics” of their code,  ensuring our infrastructure scales as efficiently as our business.

THE PURPLLE ENGINEER: MINDSET & IMPACT
We aren’t looking for “feature factories”; we are looking for owners of a resilient ecosystem. At Purplle, your success is defined by your mindset and the tangible impact you have on our platform:
** The Systems Thinker (Quality & Resilience): You hate fixing the same bug twice. You’d rather build a “self-healing” mechanism than perform a manual fix. Your goal is to maintain a Change Failure Rate of <1.5% and help us reach a state of fewer than 2 Sev-1 incidents per quarter.
** The AI-Fluent Architect (High Velocity): You are already using AI tools to 10x your own productivity. 
** You leverage these tools to drive a cycle time of <4 hours and maintain a high frequency of daily deployments, moving code from commit to production with total confidence.
** The Data-Informed Engineer (Economic Discipline): You care about the “why” behind the code. You understand that latency and cloud spend are engineering disciplines, and you optimize your infrastructure so that our cost-per-order remains predictable as we scale.
** The Proactive Collaborator (Security & Governance): You thrive in a cross-functional environment (Eng, Data, DevOps, Security). You take ownership of the “Golden Path,” ensuring 100% of your repositories are gated and that zero critical vulnerabilities ever reach our production environment.

YOUR SPECIFIC MANDATE
In this role, you will be directly responsible for:
** Ship Real Code from Week One: This is not a shadow internship. You will be assigned a real team (Backend, DevOps, Data Engineering, Android, or Data Science) and contribute to production systems within your first two weeks—with a mentor to guide you and AI tools to accelerate you.
** AI-First Engineering from Day One: You will be equipped with Cursor and Claude on day one. We expect interns to use AI tools to accelerate their learning and output. Embracing AI-assisted development isn’t optional—it’s how we work.
** Systems Thinking Bootcamp: Over six months, you will learn how unbreakable systems are built at scale—from CI/CD pipelines and Kubernetes deployments to data quality frameworks and ML model observability. You will leave thinking like a systems engineer, not just a coder.
** Cross-Functional Exposure: Work closely with engineers across Backend, Data, DevOps, and Product. Attend design reviews, incident post-mortems, and architecture discussions—understanding how decisions at every layer affect the platform’s resilience.
** Ownership & Impact: Your work will be reviewed, merged, and running in production. You will present your contributions to the broader engineering team at the end of your internship. We celebrate outcomes, not just effort.
