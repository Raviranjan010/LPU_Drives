Job Profile Details:
Designations: Business Development Associate, HR Intern, AI and  ML Engineer,  Full-Stack Developer Intern

Full-Stack Developer Intern:
** Develop and maintain web & mobile applications using React.js and React Native
** Build scalable backend services with Node.js, ExpressJS, and Socket.io
** Work with MongoDB, PostgreSQL, and Redis for data management
** Optimize applications for performance, scalability, and reliability
** Debug, test, and ensure system stability
** Collaborate with cross-functional teams to deliver high-quality products

What We Look For
** Proficiency in React.js, Node.js, Express.js
** Experience with MongoDB, PostgreSQL, Redis
** Familiarity with Socket.io and React Native
** Strong problem-solving skills & passion for full-stack development
** Pursuing or recently completed a degree in CS/Software Engineering or related field
** Knowledge of cloud services (AWS, GCP, Azure)
** Experience with Docker, CI/CD, or microservices
** Understanding of authentication & security best practices

AI & ML Engineer:
** Design, build, and deploy machine learning models to power Unlox's AI-based learning features.
** Develop AI solutions for personalized recommendations, adaptive learning content, and intelligent mentoring tools.
** Research, implement, and optimize machine learning algorithms.
** Develop, train, and fine-tune neural network architectures.
** Perform data preprocessing, feature engineering, and data analysis to improve model performance.
** Evaluate, test, and optimize machine learning models for accuracy and efficiency.
** Collaborate with Product, Engineering, and Learning Design teams to integrate AI/ML solutions into the platform.
** Ensure the scalability, reliability, and seamless integration of machine learning models.
** Monitor and maintain model performance in production environments.
** Continuously refine and improve models based on user behavior, feedback, and performance metrics.
** Stay updated with the latest advancements in Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning, particularly in the EdTech domain.
** Contribute to enhancing the overall learner experience through innovative AI- driven solutions.

Qualifications:
** Strong foundation in Computer Science and Algorithms, with the ability to design efficient, scalable solutions for real-world problems.
** Proficiency in Pattern Recognition and Neural Networks, including experience building and training deep learning models for recommendation or personalization tasks.
** Solid knowledge of Statistics and probability, with experience applying statistical methods to model evaluation, A/B testing, and performance analysis.
** Hands-on experience with common ML/AI tools and frameworks (e.g., Python, TensorFlow, PyTorch, scikit-learn) and modern data processing workflows.
** Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Computer Science, Data Science, Artificial Intelligence, or a related technical field, or equivalent practical experience.
** Ability to work collaboratively in a cross-functional team, communicate complex concepts clearly, and translate learning objectives into technical solutions.
** Experience in edtech, recommendation systems, or personalization engines is a plus, along with familiarity with cloud platforms and MLOps practices.
