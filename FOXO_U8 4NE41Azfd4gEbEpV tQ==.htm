Role: Backend Engineers (Intern) 

Role Overview:
We are looking for a Backend Engineering Intern who has already built real-world applications and wants to work on production systems at FOXO. This is not a training-only internship. We are looking for candidates who have hands-on coding experience through internships, hackathons, open-source contributions, freelance work, research projects, or personal projects. You will work closely with our engineering and product teams to build APIs, backend services, integrations, and AI-powered applications used by real customers.

What You'll Do:
** Build and maintain backend services using Python and FastAPI.
** Develop APIs powering web, mobile, and AI applications.
** Design and work with databases, caching layers, and external integrations.
** Collaborate with engineers, product managers, and designers to ship features.
** Debug production issues and improve system performance.
** Write clean, maintainable, and well-tested code.
** Participate in technical discussions and architecture reviews.

What We're Looking For:
** Python, FastAPI, SQL, DSA & Problem Solving 
** Strong programming skills in Python.
** Good understanding of APIs, databases, authentication, and backend fundamentals.
** Understanding of data structures, algorithms, and system design basics.
** Familiarity with SQL databases such as PostgreSQL or MySQL.
** Experience building and deploying backend projects.
** Strong problem-solving skills and ownership mindset.
** Ability to learn quickly and work independently.

Preferred Experience:
** Previous internship, freelance work, research project, or significant personal project.
** Experience with FastAPI, Django, Flask, or similar backend frameworks.
** Familiarity with Redis, Docker, Git, and cloud platforms.
** Exposure to AI applications, LLM APIs, or modern SaaS products.
** Participation in hackathons, coding competitions, or open-source projects.

Mandatory Application Requirements:
To be considered, applicants must share:
** GitHub profile.
** Resume.
** Links to projects, APIs, applications, or technical work.
** A short note describing the most technically challenging project they have built and their contribution.

Role: Agentic AI Engineers (Intern)

Role Overview:
FOXO is looking for an Agentic AI Engineering Intern who has already built and shipped AI applications, agents, workflows, or automation systems using modern AI tools and frameworks. This is not a research internship and not a learning-only role. We are looking for builders who have hands-on experience developing AI-powered products and can contribute from Day 1. You will work directly with our AI and Product teams to build AI agents, LLM-powered workflows, RAG systems, copilots, and automation tools that solve real-world healthcare and longevity problems.

What You'll Do:
** Build and improve AI agents, copilots, and LLM-powered applications.
** Develop RAG pipelines using vector databases and retrieval systems.
** Design and implement agent workflows, tool calling, and structured outputs.
** Build APIs and backend services supporting AI products.
** Evaluate and improve AI output quality, reliability, latency, and cost.
** Work closely with Product and Engineering teams to turn ideas into production-ready features.
** Rapidly prototype, test, and iterate on AI solutions.

What We're Looking For:
** Python, Agentic AI, RAG, LLMs & Problem Solving
** Strong hands-on experience building AI applications using LLMs.
** Demonstrated ability to build and ship working projects end-to-end.
** Strong Python development skills.
** Understanding of prompt engineering, agent design, and AI workflows.
** Comfortable working in a fast-moving startup environment.
** Ability to independently solve technical problems and execute quickly
