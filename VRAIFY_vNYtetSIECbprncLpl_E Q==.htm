Job Profile Details:

Vraify provides expert human data, reinforcement-learning environments and verifier infrastructure to help develop and evaluate frontier AI systems. Its work spans software engineering, coding, reasoning and mathematics, multilingual AI, benchmark evaluation and AI-agent training.
Vraify is partnering with universities across India to identify students and recent graduates for multilingual AI engineering projects. As a Multilingual AI Engineer Intern, you will help benchmark, evaluate and improve generative-AI systems across language quality, reasoning, coding, factuality and AI-agent workflows.
Our multilingual AI projects require careful evaluation in English and Indic languages. Native-language expertise is essential because high-quality AI evaluation depends on accurate native-script writing, cultural and linguistic nuance, and the ability to identify errors that translation tools may miss.

What you will do
Depending on your assigned project, you may:
** Execute structured LLM evaluation and benchmarking tasks using project guidelines.
** Review AI-generated answers for accuracy, quality, factuality, reasoning and instruction-following.
** Identify language, mathematical, coding, tool-use and AI-agent workflow errors.
** Compare competing AI responses, select the stronger response and document evidence-based findings.
** Evaluate multilingual AI quality in English and your native language, including native-script fluency and correctness.
** Write or improve answers in English and your native language, following reviewer feedback and quality standards.
** Contribute to benchmark coverage by surfacing repeatable model failure patterns and actionable observations.

Language requirement:
** This is a strict eligibility requirement.
** Apply only if you grew up speaking one of the listed languages and can read and write it confidently in its native script:
** Bengali, Gujarati, Hindi, Kannada, Malayalam, Marathi, Nepali, Odia, Punjabi, Tamil, Telugu or Urdu.

Please do not select a language simply because you studied it as a school subject, understand conversations or films, can speak a few sentences, can write it using Roman letters, or can produce answers using translation tools.
Shortlisted applicants will complete a language assessment, including a native-script writing task. Working English proficiency at B1–B2 level or above is also required.
Choose your subject area
You need strong ability in at least one of the following areas:
** Coding: writing, understanding, reviewing and debugging code
** Agentic AI and tool calling: working with APIs, functions, tools and multi-step AI workflows
** Mathematics: solving and reviewing university-level mathematical problems
** General knowledge: fact-checking and identifying unsupported information
** Commonsense reasoning: evaluating practical logic and cause-and-effect reasoning
** Chat and instruction following: checking whether responses correctly follow user instructions
** Reading comprehension: answering questions using evidence from a passage
** Long-context evaluation: reading long documents and connecting information across sections

Select only the areas you can support with coursework, grades, projects, research, teaching or work experience. A subject-specific assessment will be part of the selection process.

Who can apply:
** Applications are open to undergraduate and postgraduate students, final-year and pre-final-year students, recent graduates, research scholars and doctoral candidates, and faculty members with relevant expertise. Professional experience is not compulsory.
** You will need a personal computer, reliable internet access and enough weekly availability to work consistently on a remote project.

What you receive:
** Paid, project-based remote work
** Compensation: 30k-50k per month for 20-40 hours per week(Based upon performance in bootcamp).
** Hands-on experience evaluating and benchmarking real-world generative-AI systems
** Project training and reviewer feedback
** Internship certificate after satisfactory completion
** Consideration for future Vraify projects
** PPO consideration for consistently strong performers, subject to role availability
** Project allocation and workload depend on assessment results, performance and client demand.

Selection process:
** Application review
** Native-language assessment
** Subject-specific qualification test
** Project training and onboarding
