Role: HR Intern

About the Role
As an HR Intern at HUFT, you'll get a front-row seat to how people operations run in a fast-growing consumer brand. From sourcing great talent to helping new joiners feel at home from day one, you'll be part of the team that keeps HUFT's pack growing — one great hire at a time. This role is perfect for someone who's people-first, endlessly curious about what makes teams tick, and excited to learn the ropes of recruitment, onboarding, and everyday HR operations — all while working alongside (and probably being adopted by) some of Gurugram's friendliest office dogs.

What You Will Do
Recruitment & Talent Sourcing
** Assist in sourcing and screening candidates across platforms (LinkedIn, job boards, referrals).
** Schedule and coordinate interviews with candidates and hiring managers.
** Help draft and post job descriptions.
** Maintain and update candidate trackers and hiring dashboards.

Onboarding & Employee Experience
** Support new hire onboarding — documentation, induction, and welcome kits.
** Help coordinate orientation sessions for new joiners.
** Assist in planning employee engagement activities and events.

HR Operations
** Support day-to-day HR processes — attendance, documentation, and record-keeping.
** Assist with maintaining employee databases and HR trackers.
** Help prepare HR reports and presentations.

Culture & Communication
** Support internal communication and employee engagement initiatives.
** Help organise team events, celebrations, and culture-building activities.

Who This Is For
** Pursuing or recently completed a degree in HR, Business Administration or a related field.
** Genuinely excited about people, recruitment, and how organisations work.
** Basic understanding of recruitment and HR processes (coursework, projects, or prior internship experience welcome).
** Strong communication skills — you can talk to anyone, from a candidate to a CXO.
** Organised, detail-oriented, and comfortable managing multiple things at once.
** Comfortable with Excel/Google Sheets and basic presentation tools.

Bonus Points If You Have
** Prior internship or project experience in HR/recruitment.
** An interest in employer branding, culture, or people analytics.
** Love pets (you'll fit right in).

Role: Design Intern

About the Role
The Design Intern will help bring ideas to life— turning sketches, thoughts, and concepts into meaningful products and visual stories. This role sits at the heart of product design, visual storytelling, and merchandising, where creativity directly shapes how ideas are experienced in the real world. At HUFT, design goes beyond aesthetics — it connects deeply with pets and pet parents, shaping products and experiences that improve the lives of the animals we love. You'll work closely with the design team to explore product ideas, build visual directions, and develop designs that are thoughtful, functional, and emotionally engaging. We're looking for someone who is curious about how things are made, excited by aesthetics, and eager to experiment, learn, and create.

What You Will Do
Concept to Design
** Translate ideas into early sketches, concepts, and product explorations that evolve into tangible design directions.
** Support product design development through mock-ups, iterations, and visual storytelling.

Visual & Brand Assets
** Create high-quality design assets using Adobe Photoshop and Illustrator for product and brand needs.
** Develop illustrations, layouts, mood boards, and presentation boards that bring ideas to life visually.

Merchandising & Product Storytelling
** Assist in shaping product stories for collections designed for pets and pet parents through strong merchandising and visual thinking.
** Help translate design concepts into retail-ready visuals that feel cohesive, engaging, and on-brand for a pet-first lifestyle brand.

Trends & Collaboration
** Stay inspired by tracking design trends, materials, colours, and cultural references, especially in pet lifestyle and consumer products.
** Collaborate with cross-functional teams to execute fast-paced creative work with clarity and attention to detail.

Who This Is For
** Currently pursuing or recently graduated in Product Design, Fashion Design, Graphic Design,
** Visual Communication, Industrial Design, or a related field.
** Strong hands-on skills in Adobe Photoshop and Illustrator.
** Good understanding of product design thinking and the visual development process.
** Strong aesthetic sense with an eye for composition, colour, typography, and detail.
** Ability to sketch, visualise, and communicate ideas clearly through design.
** Interest in products, design systems, merchandising, and visual storytelling.
** Highly curious, observant, and open to learning in a fast-moving creative environment.

Bonus Points If You Have
** A genuine love for pets and interest in pet lifestyle products.
** A portfolio of academic or personal design work.
