Associate, Business Development

At Kraftshala, we help students and young professionals launch their careers in marketing, sales. and beyond. As an Associate, Business Development, you'll be the first point of contact for students considering our programs.

You'll understand what students are aiming for and help them decide whether the course is the right next step for their careers. Your day will involve explaining the program in a simple, honest way, answering their questions, following up so they don't miss deadlines, and keeping your pipeline organised. You'll also share insights with our marketing team so we can keep improving how we reach students like them.

But what truly defines success in this role is straightforward. There are just three metrics that matter:
** How effectively you convert your leads into enrolments
** How many students you enrol in each batch
** The monthly revenue your enrolments contribute

** Everything you do - every conversation, follow-up, and insight - directly helps you move these three numbers. And as these grow, so does your career, your learning, and your earning potential at Kraftshala.

Monthly Outcomes:
** Month 1: Learn the ropes - understand our programs, observe live calls, and start closing your first few enrolments with guidance.
** Month 2: Run independent calls, handle objections confidently, and start hitting your individual enrollment targets.
** Month 3+: Own your sales pipeline end-to-end - consistently close enrolments, exceed revenue goals, and share insights to make our process sharper.

Top Grading:

A-Player (Great fit):
** You communicate clearly and make complex program details easy to understand.
** You stay disciplined - always following up with students and keeping your work organized.
** You stay motivated and keep improving your approach, aiming to do better each month.
** You take ownership, not waiting to be told what to do, but finding ways to help students and the team succeed.

B-Player (Not the right fit):
** You struggle to explain things in a simple and clear way.
** You miss follow-ups or forget to update your work, letting students slip away.
** You regularly fall short of your goals without trying to learn or improve.
** You wait for constant instructions instead of taking charge of your own growth.

Must Haves:
** Strong communication skills in English (spoken and written).
** Comfort of working with targets and deadlines.
** Basic computer skills (using email, spreadsheets, or simple tracking tools).
** Motivation to build a career in admissions/sales/education.

Good-to-Haves:
** Prior internship or work experience in sales, admissions, or counseling.
** Experience in student leadership, volunteering, or side projects that show initiative.

Senior Associate, Program Management - Student Operations & Experience

As a Senior Associate, Program Management - Student Operations & Experience at Kraftshala, you'll coordinate live sessions, manage stakeholders, improve processes, and ensure our programs run smoothly at scale. You will own operational execution across multiple moving parts.

This role requires someone who can combine strong operational thinking with empathy, responsiveness, attention to detail, and excellent execution discipline.

Specific responsibilities will include:

Program Management & Student Operations
** Taking charge of the entire learning journey for assigned student cohorts and ensuring smooth end-to-end program delivery. Metric to Measure: % of students achieving key learning milestones: cohort completion metrics
** Being the first point of contact for students, solving queries promptly, empathetically, and with strong ownership. Metric to Measure: Average response/resolution time; student satisfaction scores
** Coordinating and executing live sessions, schedules, assessments, and operational workflows seamlessly across batches. Metric to Measure: % of sessions conducted without operational issues or reschedules
** Managing student escalations effectively and ensuring timely closure while maintaining a strong student experience. Metric to Measure: Escalation turnaround time; escalation satisfaction scores
** Tracking student engagement, participation, and progress using internal systems and operational dashboards. Metric to Measure: % of students receiving timely nudges/support; engagement metrics

Stakeholder Management & Cross-functional Coordination
** Liaising with trainers, mentors, evaluators, operations teams, and internal stakeholders to ensure aligned execution. Metric to Measure: Timeliness and quality of stakeholder coordination; stakeholder feedback
** Managing communication across multiple stakeholders while ensuring clarity. responsiveness, and follow-through. Metric to Measure: Task completion timelines; communication effectiveness

Process Improvement & Operational Excellence
** Identifying inefficiencies in workflows and proactively improving operational systems and processes. Metric to Measure: Number of process improvements implemented; measurable impact on delivery quality
Leveraging tools and automation platforms (Notion, Google Sheets, Zapier, etc.) to streamline workflows and reduce manual effort. Metric to Measure: Reduction in manual interventions, automation coverage across workflows

Monthly Outcomes:
** Month 1: Learn the ropes - understand our program delivery workflows, student journey, systems, tools, communication practices, and operational processes while shadowing ongoing cohorts.
** Month 2-3: Take ownership of one or more student cohorts end-to-end, independently manage student communication and opprational workflows, and identify at least one meaningful process improvement opportunity.
** Month 4+: Own multiple cohorts independently, handle stakeholder coordination smoothly, drive operational execution with minimal supervision, and consistently hit key program delivery metrics.

Top Grading:
** Ownership & Accountability: An A-Player takes complete ownership of outcomes, follows through consistently, and proactively solves problems before they escalate, whereas a B-Player requires reminders and reacts only after issues surface.
** Operational Excellence: An A-Player brings structure to complexity, manages multiple moving parts effectively, and executes reliably under pressure, whereas a B-Player struggles with prioritization and consistency.
** Student Obsession: An A-Player genuinely cares about student experience and looks for ways to improve it proactively, whereas a -Player focuses only on task completion.
** Process Thinking: An A-Player continuously improves systems and workflows to make operations more scalable and efficient, whereas a B-Player sticks to existing processes without questioning or optimizing them.
** Attention to Detail: An A-Player catches small issues early and maintains high execution accuracy, whereas a B-Player misses details that create operational friction or delays.
** Communication & Coordination: An A-Player communicates clearly, promptly, and with context across stakeholders, whereas a B-Player's communication is inconsistent, delayed, or incomplete.
** Learning Agility: An A-Player adapts quickly, absorbs feedback well, and improves continuously, whereas a B-Player struggles with change or repeated feedback.

Must Haves:
** Strong execution discipline and the ability to manage work independently in fast-paced environments
** Experience handling multiple stakeholders, timelines, and operational workflows simultaneously
** Strong written and verbal communication skills
** Comfort with ambiguity, problem-solving, and operational firefighting when needed
** Hands-on proficiency with tools like Google Sheets, MS Excel, Zoom, Notion, or similar workflow/project management tools
** High responsiveness, reliability, and attention to detail

Good-to-Haves:
** Prior experience in customer-facing roles, customer support, customer success, student engagement, or stakeholder management.
** Teaching, training, mentoring, tutoring, or facilitation experience would be an added advantage.

Senior Associate, Channel Partnership

The Senior Associate, Channel Partnerships will own on-ground partnerships with colleges, universities, and training institutes to drive awareness and enrolments for Kraftshala's placement-oriented programs. This is a field-heavy, individual contributor role requiring frequent travel, strong stakeholder management, and sharp execution.

While the initial focus will be Delhi NCR, deployment will be opportunity-led with pan-India scope over time.

Specific Responsibilities & Metrics:
** Build and manage institutional partnerships. Metrics to measure: Number of active college/institute partnerships; depth of engagement per partner; repeat collaborations
** Plan and execute on-ground seminars, workshops, and events. Metrics to measure:
** Number of events conducted; attendance quality; lead yield per event
** Generate and hand over qualified leads to the Admissions team. Metrics to measure:
** Leads generated; lead qualification rate; successful handover and follow-through
** Own relationships with TPOs, deans, and faculty stakeholders. Metrics to measure:
** Stakeholder responsiveness; partnership longevity; referral introductions
** Track performance and pipeline using CRM tools. Metrics to measure: CRM hygiene; reporting accuracy; visibility of funnel and outcomes

Monthly Outcomes:

Month 1:
** Onboard fully on Kraftshala's programs, value proposition, and admissions funnel
** Activate existing college/institute contacts
** Close initial partnerships and execute early seminars/events

Month 2:
** Build a predictable cadence of events and partnerships
** Improve lead quality and handover effectiveness
** Identify high-performing partner profiles and formats

Month 3+:
** Scale partnerships across new geographies based on opportunity
** Drive consistent contribution to enrolments and revenue
** Act as a feedback loop to leadership on on-ground GTM insights

Top Grading:
** Ownership & Accountability: An A-player independently builds and scales partnerships end-to-end, whereas a B-player waits for direction and external momentum.

** Execution Excellence: An A-player consistently converts on-ground activity into qualified leads, whereas a E-player confuses effort with outcomes.
** Data Discipline: An A-player maintains clean CRM data and clear reporting, whereas a B-player treats tracking as an afterthought.

Must Haves:
