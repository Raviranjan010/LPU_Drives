Job Profile Details:

Job Title: Network Expansion Team – Project Manager
Location: Pan India / Multi-Location

Job Summary:
We are looking for a proactive and technically strong Network Expansion Team member with a Civil/Architectural background to manage multiple showroom and workshop expansion projects across India. The role will involve independently managing 10–15 projects simultaneously across multiple geographies/states, from site identification and finalization through execution and handover. The candidate will be responsible for coordinating with contractors, consultants, vendors, landlords, and Brand/OEM teams to ensure projects are delivered within timelines, budget, and quality standards.

Key Responsibilities:
1. Network Expansion & Site Finalization
** Identify, evaluate and finalize suitable sites for new showrooms/workshops.
** Coordinate site surveys, feasibility studies, measurements and preliminary requirements.
** Support commercial discussions and documentation for new locations.

2. Project & Site Management:
** Independently manage 10–15 projects simultaneously across different locations.
** Monitor civil, architectural, interiors and related site activities.
** Ensure projects are executed as per approved drawings, specifications, timelines and quality standards.
** Conduct regular site visits and resolve execution-level issues.

3. Contractor & Vendor Management:
** Identify, coordinate and manage contractors, vendors and consultants.
** Obtain quotations, compare proposals and support negotiations.
** Track contractor performance, project costs, schedules and deliverables.

4. OEM / Brand Coordination:
** Act as the key coordination point between RAAMGROUP projects and respective OEM/Brand teams.
** Ensure compliance with brand standards, drawings, approvals and project requirements.
** Coordinate inspections, approvals and timely closure of OEM observations.

5. Project Tracking & Reporting:
** Maintain project schedules, budgets, BOQs, progress reports and documentation.
** Provide regular project updates to the Project Head / Management.
** Highlight project risks, delays and critical decisions proactively.

Qualifications & Skills:
** Civil Engineering / Architecture background preferred.
** Understanding in site execution, project management, construction or real-estate development preferred.
** Interest to independently manage multiple projects across different locations.
** Strong contractor, vendor and stakeholder management skills.
** Good working knowledge of MS Excel, Word, PowerPoint
** Willingness to travel extensively across India.

Desired Attributes:
** Highly proactive, accountable and execution-oriented.
** Strong problem-solving and negotiation skills.
** Ability to manage multiple priorities under tight timelines.
** Excellent communication and coordination skills.
** Comfortable working independently across different geographies.
** Strong ownership mindset with a “project closure” approach.

Skills Required:
Good Communication Skills
