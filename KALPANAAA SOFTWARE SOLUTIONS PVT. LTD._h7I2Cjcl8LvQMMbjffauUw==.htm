Job Profile Details:
Designation: Software Trainee Engineer

Position Overview
** We are looking for enthusiastic and passionate Software trainee Engineers who are eager to build a strong career in software development. 
** This internship provides practical exposure to real-time projects under the guidance of experienced developers.
** Interns will work on live development tasks, participate in project discussions, write production-quality code, and learn industry-standard software development practices.
** Candidates who demonstrate excellent technical skills, discipline, and consistent performance during the internship may be offered a full-time role.

Salary Description:

Roles & Responsibilities
The selected Software Developer Intern will:
** Develop and maintain web and software applications.
** Write clean, efficient, and reusable code.
** Participate in software design discussions.
** Fix bugs and improve application performance.
** Work with databases and REST APIs.
** Develop responsive user interfaces.
** Assist in backend development.
** Perform testing and debugging.
** Prepare technical documentation.
** Follow coding standards and best practices.
** Participate in daily team meetings.
** Collaborate with designers, testers, and project managers.
** Learn new technologies as required by the project.
