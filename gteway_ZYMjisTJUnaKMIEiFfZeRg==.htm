Role Overview
As an Engineer– Software Development at Gateway Group of Companies, you will support users building software with AI tools by helping them troubleshoot issues, review AI-generated code, and transform ideas into functional products. You will provide technical guidance across development, integrations, deployment, automation, and product improvement while ensuring a seamless user experience.

Key Responsibilities
** Diagnose and resolve issues related to web applications, APIs, databases, integrations, deployments, and AI-generated code.
** Convert user requirements into clear technical solutions, implementation plans, and actionable steps.
** Review AI-generated code for bugs, security concerns, performance issues, and implementation gaps.
** Support users working with AI development tools such as ChatGPT, Claude, Cursor, Replit, Bolt, v0, Windsurf, and GitHub Copilot.
** Assist with application deployment on platforms such as AWS, Azure, GCP, Vercel, Netlify, Firebase, and Supabase.
** Work across modern technology stacks including JavaScript/TypeScript, React, Next.js, Node.js, Python, Django, Flask, PHP, Laravel, or similar technologies.
** Support API integrations, authentication, databases, DevOps basics, and third-party tools.
** Create and maintain technical documentation, FAQs, troubleshooting guides, and support playbooks.
** Collaborate with engineering, product, and operations teams to enhance user experience and support processes.

Required Skills
** Strong communication and problem-solving skills.
** Hands-on experience with at least one modern development stack.
** Good understanding of frontend, backend, APIs, databases, Git/GitHub, deployment, and cloud platforms.
** Ability to understand, debug, and improve AI-generated code.
** Familiarity with AI-assisted development tools and modern software development practices.
** Basic understanding of UI/UX, security, authentication, and production readiness.
** Ability to explain technical concepts clearly to both technical and non-technical users.
** Experience in software development, technical support, solution engineering, developer support, or product support.
** Exposure to automation tools, no-code/low-code platforms, and startup/MVP environments is an advantage.

What We Look For
** Strong technical fundamentals
** Critical thinking and structured problem-solving
** Ownership mindset and user-first approach
** Quick learning ability and adaptability
** Clear communication and collaboration skills
** Interest in AI-driven software development
