About Company:

Cargill, Incorporated is a massive, privately held global food and agricultural corporation based in Minnetonka, Minnesota, and founded in 1865 by William Wallace Cargill. As the largest privately held corporation in the United States by revenue, the company operates as a major global supplier, processor, and distributor of agricultural commodities like grains, oilseeds, sugar, and meat, while also providing financial services, risk management, and industrial products. With a vast workforce of over 160,000 employees operating across roughly 70 countries, Cargill acts as a critical link in the global food supply chain, connecting farmers with markets and producing ingredients that feed millions of people daily worldwide.
