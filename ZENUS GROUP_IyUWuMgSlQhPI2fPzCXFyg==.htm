Job Profile Details:
Job Description: Design Engineer

Role Overview
A Design Engineer designs and analyzes tower structures used in industries such as telecommunications, power transmission, broadcasting, and renewable energy. The engineer ensures towers meet structural safety standards, project specifications, and regulatory requirements.

Key Responsibilities
** Design telecommunication towers, monopoles, guyed towers, and lattice towers.
** Perform structural analysis and load calculations (wind load, seismic load, equipment load).
** Prepare engineering drawings and design reports.
** Use structural analysis software such as STAAD.Pro, AutoCAD, Tekla Structures, or RISA-3D.
** Review foundation design and soil reports for tower stability.
** Ensure compliance with standards like Telecommunications Industry Association guidelines (e.g., TIA-222).
** Coordinate with civil engineers, project managers, and site teams.
** Optimize tower designs for cost efficiency and material usage.
** Prepare bill of materials (BOM) and design documentation.

Job Description: Installation Engineer

Role Overview
A Tower Installation Engineer is responsible for the installation, erection, and maintenance of telecom towers and related equipment used in mobile communication networks. The engineer ensures that towers are installed safely, correctly, and according to technical and structural standards.

Key Responsibilities
** Supervise and perform telecom tower installation and erection.
** Install antennas, microwave dishes, RF cables, and telecom equipment on towers.
** Coordinate with RF engineers for proper antenna alignment and configuration.
** Ensure compliance with safety standards during tower climbing and installation.
** Inspect tower structures and verify structural stability.
** Work with telecom infrastructure companies such as Indus Towers, American Tower Corporation, and Bharti Infratel.
** Install and test telecom equipment from vendors like Ericsson, Nokia, and Huawei.
** Perform site surveys and coordinate with civil teams for foundation and tower erection work.
** Troubleshoot installation issues and ensure proper system functioning.

Description: RF Engineer (Radio Frequency Engineer)

Role Overview
An RF Engineer designs, tests, and maintains radio frequency communication systems used in wireless technologies such as mobile networks, satellite communication, broadcasting, and radar systems. The role focuses on optimizing signal performance, coverage, and network capacity.

Key Responsibilities
** Design and optimize wireless communication systems and RF circuits.
** Perform RF planning and network optimization for cellular networks.
** Conduct drive tests and signal measurements to evaluate network performance.
** Analyze parameters such as signal strength, interference, and coverage.
** Work with telecom technologies like 4G LTE and 5G NR.
** Use RF planning and analysis tools such as Atoll, TEMS Investigation, and Mentum Planet.
** Optimize antenna placement and tower configurations.
** Troubleshoot signal interference and coverage issues.
** Coordinate with network planning, field engineers, and tower teams.

Job Description: Telecom Network Analyst Engineer

Role Overview
A Telecom Network Analyst Engineer monitors, analyzes, and optimizes telecommunications networks to ensure high performance, reliability, and quality of service. The role focuses on analysing network data, identifying issues, and improving network efficiency for technologies such as 4G LTE and 5G NR.

Key Responsibilities
** Monitor telecom network performance and identify network faults or congestion.
** Analyze KPIs (Key Performance Indicators) such as call drop rate, latency, and throughput.
** Generate network performance reports and analytics.
** Investigate and resolve network performance issues.
** Work with RF, core, and transmission teams to optimize network quality.
** Use telecom monitoring and analytics tools such as SolarWinds Network Performance Monitor, Wireshark, and NetAct.
** Track alarms and faults using Network Operation Center (NOC) systems.
** Support network upgrades and configuration changes
