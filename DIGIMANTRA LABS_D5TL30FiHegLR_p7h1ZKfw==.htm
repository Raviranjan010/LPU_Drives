Job Profile Details:
Description:
** We are excited to introduce the 8ucate Campus Ambassador Programme, an opportunity designed to help students build leadership skills, gain industry exposure, strengthen their professional networks, and develop practical experience alongside their academic journey.
** We would appreciate your help in sharing this opportunity with students at your institution who are enthusiastic, proactive, and interested in taking on leadership responsibilities.

Why Students Should Join
** Leadership Experience: Develop communication, coordination, and leadership skills.
** Industry Exposure : Get closer to real-world industry trends, professionals, and opportunities.
** Professional Networking: Connect with peers, industry professionals, and the wider 8ucate community.
** Recognition & Rewards:  Earn exciting rewards and recognition based on participation and performance.
** Career Development:Build valuable skills and experiences that can strengthen their professional profile.

Who Can Become an 8ucate Student Ambassador?
** We're looking for enthusiastic, proactive students who are ready to grow beyond the classroom, build valuable connections, and create meaningful opportunities within their college community.

You'll be a great fit if you:
** Are currently enrolled in a college or university
** Want to develop leadership, communication and organisational skills
** Are curious about AI, technology and future-ready careers
** Take initiative and enjoy bringing ideas to life
** Love connecting with people and building communities
** Are interested in building your personal brand and professional network
** Are active on LinkedIn, Instagram or other social platforms
** Want to make a positive impact on your campus while growing personally and professionally

Your Role as an 8ucate Student Ambassador:
** From organising campus experiences to connecting students with valuable opportunities, you'll play an active role in building a vibrant AI learning community, all while balancing your studies.
** Build Campus Connections: Connect with classmates, clubs and student communities to help more students discover valuable learning opportunities.
** Share Opportunities: Keep your campus informed about upcoming workshops, competitions, certifications and exclusive student initiatives through social media and student communities.
** Grow the Community: Welcome new students into the 8ucate community and help create an inclusive, collaborative learning environment.
** Organise Engaging Experiences: Host or support workshops, meetups, expert sessions and campus activities alongside the 8ucate team.
** Create & Share Content: Capture photos, videos and stories from campus activities to showcase your ambassador journey and inspire others.
** Lead by Example: Encourage curiosity, collaboration, and continuous learning while becoming a recognised student leader on your campus.
** Inspire & Guide Students: Help fellow students explore programmes, events and career-focused learning opportunities that match their interests.
** Represent the Student Community: Be the bridge between your campus and 8ucate by sharing ideas, feedback and student perspectives to help us create even better experiences.
Skills Required:
Good Communication Skills.
