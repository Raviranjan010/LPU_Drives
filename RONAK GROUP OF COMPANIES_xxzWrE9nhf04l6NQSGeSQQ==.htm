Job Profile Details:

Business Development Manager (B2B Wholesale):
** Identify and develop new B2B wholesale business opportunities in the assigned West African market. 
** Build and manage relationships with wholesalers, distributors, dealers, and business partners. 
** Generate leads, conduct business discussions, and convert opportunities into sales. 
** Understand customer requirements and provide appropriate product/service solutions. 
** Develop and execute sales strategies to achieve revenue and growth targets. 
** Manage the complete sales cycle from lead generation to order closure. 
** Negotiate commercial terms, pricing, and business agreements with clients. 
** Conduct market research to identify new opportunities, customer trends, and competitor activities. 
** Expand distribution networks and strengthen market presence. 
** Coordinate with internal teams for order management, supply chain, and customer support. 
** Maintain regular communication with existing clients to ensure customer satisfaction and repeat business. 
** Prepare sales reports, market insights, and business performance updates.

Business Development Manager - B2C Retail:
** Identify and develop new B2C retail business opportunities in the assigned West African market. 
** Build and maintain relationships with retail partners, distributors, and customers. 
** Understand customer needs and provide suitable product/service solutions. 
** Achieve sales targets through effective planning and market execution. 
** Conduct market research to identify trends, customer preferences, and competitor activities. 
** Support expansion of retail presence and improve brand visibility. 
** Manage customer interactions and ensure high customer satisfaction. 
** Coordinate with internal teams for smooth order processing and service delivery. 
** Prepare sales reports, market updates, and business performance analysis. 
** Participate in promotional activities, exhibitions, and customer engagement initiatives.
 
Accounts cum Warehouse Executive:
** Manage and maintain accurate inventory records and stock reports. 
** Monitor stock levels and ensure availability of required products. 
** Track inbound and outbound inventory movements.
** Coordinate with warehouse teams for receiving, storage, and dispatch activities. 
** Perform regular stock reconciliation and identify inventory discrepancies. 
** Maintain proper documentation related to stock movement and inventory transactions. 
** Prepare inventory reports and share updates with management. 
** Support demand planning and stock requirement analysis. 
** Coordinate with sales, purchase, and logistics teams for smooth operations. 
** Monitor slow-moving, fast-moving, and non-moving inventory. 
** Ensure proper inventory control processes are followed. 
** Assist in improving inventory accuracy and operational efficiency.

Retail Sales & Operations Executive:
** Manage retail sales activities and assist customers throughout the buying process. 
** Understand customer requirements and provide suitable product recommendations. 
** Support achievement of store sales targets through effective customer engagement. 
** Handle billing operations, invoice generation, and transaction processing. 
** Manage and operate the cash counter efficiently, handling cash, card, and digital payment transactions with accuracy. 
** Maintain daily sales records, billing documents, and transaction reports while ensuring proper cash handling and reconciliation. 
** Ensure accuracy in financial records, manage customer billing activities, and coordinate with inventory and sales teams to maintain product availability and smooth store operations. 
** Provide excellent customer service and build positive customer relationships.
** Prepare daily sales and operational reports. Ensure compliance with company processes related to sales, billing, and customer handling. 
** Support overall retail store operations and business growth initiatives.

** Ability to work independently in a challenging market environment

About Company:

Ronak Group is a diversified business conglomerate headquartered in Vadodara, Gujarat, established in 1976, with a strong presence in international trade, manufacturing, procurement, exports, and global business development. Serving clients across Asia, Africa, the Middle East, Europe, and other international markets, the group provides end-to-end sourcing, supply chain, import-export, healthcare, consumer goods, industrial and engineering solutions, turnkey projects, financial services, and strategic business partnerships. Ronak Group also operates textile and apparel manufacturing businesses, including uniform manufacturing, fabric supply, and corporate apparel, offering integrated solutions under one roof. With a commitment to quality, innovation, customer satisfaction, and timely delivery, the company continues to expand its global footprint while delivering customized business and market expansion solutions.
