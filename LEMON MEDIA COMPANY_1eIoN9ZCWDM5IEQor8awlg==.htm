Position Title: Website Developer

Job Responsibilities

Website Development
** Convert UI/UX designs (Figma, Adobe XD, etc.) into responsive, pixel-perfect web pages.
** Develop custom websites using modern technologies (HTML, CSS, JavaScript, React, Vue.js, Angular, etc.).
** ** Build and customize websites on CMS platforms such as WordPress, Webflow, Shopify, or custom CMS solutions.
** Create editable templates and modules that allow non-developers (designers, marketers) to manage content independently.
** Ensure responsive design and cross-browser compatibility across all devices and browsers.

Technical Implementation & Maintenance
** Implement UI/UX designs with pixel-perfect accuracy.
** Optimize website performance, including load time, image compression, and lazy loading.
** Implement SEO best practices, including structured data, clean URLs, and metadata.
** Integrate third-party APIs, plugins, and tools as required.
** Test websites across multiple browsers and devices to ensure consistent functionality and usability.
** Identify and fix bugs, broken links, layout issues, and integration errors.
** Troubleshoot security vulnerabilities and performance issues.
** Manage updates to plugins, CMS versions, and site security.
** Monitor and resolve downtime, broken features, or user experience issues.
** Maintain and update existing websites on an ongoing basis.

Hosting, Deployment & Security
** Set up and manage web hosting environments, staging servers, and domain configurations.
** Deploy websites live and ensure a seamless handoff to clients or internal teams.
** Set up basic security protocols, including SSL, firewalls, and anti-spam measures.
** Apply secure coding practices to prevent vulnerabilities such as injection attacks and data leaks.

Collaboration & Strategy
** Collaborate with content strategists, designers, and digital marketers to implement copy, assets, and functional elements based on campaign needs.
** Provide technical guidance and recommendations on current web development trends.
** Assist with website hosting, domain setup, and server management as needed.

** Knowledge of eCommerce development (Shopify, WooCommerce, Magento) is an advantage.
** Excellent problem-solving and troubleshooting skills.
** Strong communication and collaboration skills.
