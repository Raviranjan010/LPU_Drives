Job Profile Details:

Role Overview:
As a Developer Relations (DevRel) Specialist at Keploy, you will be at the forefront of building our DevRel team from scratch. You’ll work directly with Neha gupta (Founder) and help us engage with developers, build relationships, create valuable content, and advocate for our products in the world of AI, testing, and APIs. This is a unique, hands-on opportunity to make a significant impact on a fast-growing company while learning and growing in the DevRel space.

What You’ll Do:
** Engage the Developer Community: Build strong relationships with developers, SDETs, and QA engineers by advocating for Keploy’s products in testing, AI, and API spaces.
** Content Creation & Communication: Create technical content (blogs, videos, tutorials) and deliver talks/webinars to educate and inspire the developer community.
** Collaborate & Educate: Work closely with the product, marketing, and engineering teams to advocate for developers' needs and bring their feedback to improve Keploy’s products.
** Build Partnerships: Identify opportunities to partner with other key organizations in the developer ecosystem and help establish Keploy as a thought leader in AI-driven testing.
** Develop Resources: Build and maintain helpful resources such as documentation, FAQs, and best practices that will empower developers to get the most out of Keploy.

What We’re Looking For:
** Decent Coding Experience: You have hands-on experience writing code, ideally in Go, Python, or JavaScript, and an understanding of testing frameworks and automation.
** Passion for Testing, AI, and APIs: You are excited about the intersection of AI, testing, and APIs and eager to share your passion with others.
** Strong Communication Skills: You’re confident in speaking to developers, writing technical content, and presenting on various platforms.
** Self-Starter & Learner: You’re proactive, self-driven, and love learning new things while contributing to building a great team.
** Prior DevRel Experience (Nice to Have): Previous experience in developer relations, advocacy, or community management is a bonus, but not required.

About Company:

Keploy is an AI-powered testing platform designed to help developers save time by automating unit test generation, API tests, and more. We’re transforming the testing space by combining cutting-edge AI with developer-focused solutions, and we’re looking for a passionate DevRel Specialist to join us on this exciting journey. Keploy is an open-source, AI-driven code verification and test generation platform that converts real user and production API traffic into comprehensive test cases and data mocks. Founded in 2022, it aims to eliminate manual test case writing and prevent regressions in backend applications.
