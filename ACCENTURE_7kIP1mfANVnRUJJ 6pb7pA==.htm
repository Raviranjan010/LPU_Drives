Job Profile Details:
1. PAN Card is Mandatory
** Every student must possess a valid PAN Card.
** The photograph on the PAN Card and Aadhaar Card should match.
** Any discrepancy during verification may lead to disqualification.

2. Name Consistency Across Documents
The following names must be identical:
** Academic Mark Sheets
** PAN Card
** Accenture Registration Form
** Even a small mismatch in spelling, initials or sequence of names can make a student ineligible for the process.

3. Internship Declaration
** Students should exercise extreme caution while filling this section.
If they declare an internship, they must be able to produce:
** Offer Letter
** Internship Certificate
** Salary Slips (where applicable)
** Any other supporting documents
If they have not completed a genuine internship, it is advisable not to mention one.
The Accenture team has explicitly clarified that internships do not provide any salary advantage or fast-track consideration during the hiring process. Therefore, students should only declare internships that can be fully substantiated.


4. Relevant Certifications
Students should preferably highlight certifications only in:
** Salesforce
** ServiceNow
** Pega
