Job Profile Details:

Designations: Data Analyst, Flutter Developer, Event Coordinator, Social Media Manager, Outreach Executive, Sales Executive, Implementation Manager

Implementation Manager:
** As an Implementation Manager, you will own the client onboarding journey — deploying Medoc Health’s software at hospitals and clinics, training users, and driving successful go-lives. 
** You will be the primary bridge between clients and internal teams, ensuring each implementation is smooth, on time, and sets the client up for long-term success.

Key Responsibilities.
** Manage end-to-end onboarding and deployment of Medoc’s software for new clients.
** Configure and set up the product to match each client’s workflow and requirements.
** Train hospital and clinic staff on effective use of the platform.
** Plan and coordinate go-lives, tracking timelines and milestones.
** Act as the primary point of contact between clients and internal teams during implementation.
** Troubleshoot issues and coordinate resolutions with technical

Social Media Manager:
** As a Social Media Manager, you will build and grow Medoc Health’s presence across social platforms. 
** You will plan content calendars, create engaging posts, run campaigns, and track performance — shaping how the healthcare community perceives and engages with the Medoc brand.
 Key Responsibilities:
** Plan and manage content calendars across Instagram, LinkedIn, Facebook, and other platforms.
** Create, schedule, and publish engaging posts, reels, and stories.
** Write clear, on-brand copy and coordinate with design for creatives.
** Grow followers, engagement, and reach through organic and paid campaigns.
** Monitor comments, messages, and mentions; engage with the community.
** Track performance metrics and prepare periodic analytics reports.
** Stay current with social trends, formats, and platform updates.

Outreach Executive:
** As an Outreach Executive, you will build and nurture relationships with hospitals, clinics, and healthcare partners to expand Medoc Health’s network. 
** You will identify prospects, initiate conversations, and act as the bridge between the field and internal teams to drive partnerships and awareness.

Key Responsibilities:
** Identify and reach out to potential partners — hospitals, clinics, and healthcare professionals.
** Initiate and maintain relationships with key stakeholders and decision-makers.
** Conduct calls, meetings, and follow-ups to move prospects forward.
** Explain Medoc’s offerings and align them to partner needs.
** Collect market and partner feedback and share it with internal teams.
** Travel within the assigned region for meetings and outreach activities.

Event Coordinator:
** As an Event Coordinator, you will plan and execute Medoc Health’s events — from medical camps, product launches, and webinars to conferences and internal engagement activities. 
** You will own logistics end to end, coordinate with vendors and internal teams, and ensure every event runs smoothly and reflects the Medoc brand.

Key Responsibilities:
** Plan, organize, and execute on-ground and virtual events end to end
** Coordinate logistics — venue, vendors, travel, materials, and setup.
** Liaise with internal teams, speakers, partners, and external vendors.
** Manage event budgets and track expenses against plan.
** Prepare timelines, checklists, and run-sheets to keep events on schedule.
** Handle on-site coordination and troubleshoot issues in real time.
** Collect post-event feedback and prepare summary reports.

Flutter Developer:
** As a Flutter Developer, you will help build and maintain Medoc Health’s mobile applications using Flutter (Dart). 
** You will work on UI/UX implementation and feature development, collaborate with the development team on live projects, and grow into a full-stack mobile contributor through hands-on, real-world work.

Key Responsibilities:
** Develop and maintain mobile applications using Flutter (Dart).
** Implement UI/UX designs and build new features.
** Debug, test, and optimize application performance.
** Integrate REST APIs and work with app architecture.
** Collaborate with the development team on ongoing projects.
** Participate in code reviews and team discussions.
** Deliver assigned tasks within deadlines.

Area Sales Executive:
** As a Sales Executive, you will drive Medoc Health’s business growth by generating leads, demonstrating products to healthcare professionals, and closing sales within your assigned territory. 
** This is a field-facing role that rewards consistent activity, strong follow-through, and target achievement.

Key Responsibilities:
** Conduct field visits to hospitals, clinics, and doctor chambers within the assigned territory.
** Present and demonstrate Medoc’s product suite to doctors, administrators, and key stakeholders.
** Generate, nurture, and convert leads throughout the sales cycle.
** Meet and exceed weekly and monthly sales targets. 
** Maintain accurate records of leads, interactions, and conversions in CRM systems.
** Follow up on payments and outstanding dues where applicable.
** Share market feedback with internal teams for product and strategy improvement.

Data Analyst:
** As a Data Analyst, you will collect, clean, and interpret data to support business decisions across Medoc Health. 
** Working closely with cross-functional teams, you will turn raw datasets — including large data received from client-side sources — into clear insights, dashboards, and reports that drive product and operational improvements.

Key Responsibilities
** Collect, clean, and analyze structured and unstructured data. 
** Use Python (Pandas, NumPy) for data analysis and processing. 
** Build reports, dashboards, and visualizations to communicate insights clearly. 
** Identify trends, patterns, and actionable insights from data. 
** Handle and curate large client-side datasets, ensuring data quality and consistency. 
** Perform data validation and quality checks. 
** Assist in building and maintaining basic data pipelines and automating repetitive tasks. 
** Work with cross-functional teams to understand and translate data requirements.
