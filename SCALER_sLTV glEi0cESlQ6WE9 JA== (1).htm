Job Profile Details:

Why this internship is different
** Most internships hand you a well-defined ticket in a well-defined codebase. This one hands you a problem that did not have a good solution before LLMs existed. Scaler is rebuilding its product surface to be AI-native — not AI bolted onto an existing flow, but products whose core loop is a model doing work a human used to do. An AI teaching assistant that resolves a learner's doubt at 2 AM. Agents that review code submissions and give feedback with a mentor's judgment. Systems that read thousands of hours of counselor conversations and tell us what learners are actually confused about. Evaluation harnesses that tell us whether any of it is getting better. This is genuinely unsolved work, and it moves fast enough that a six-month intern can own a piece of it end to end. You will not be shadowing someone. You will ship to production, in front of real learners, in your first few weeks.

What you'll work on
** You'll be placed on one of the teams based on your interests and strengths. Every track involves building with LLMs — the difference is what you're pointing them at.

Core Product & Platform
** The learning platform itself: classes, assignments, code submissions, mentorship. You might build the AI teaching assistant that handles doubt resolution, an agentic code-review system that gives learners feedback in seconds instead of days, or the retrieval layer that grounds all of it in our actual curriculum.

AI Products
** Net-new product bets. Multi-agent workflows, prompt and context engineering, RAG pipelines, fine-tuning where it earns its keep, and the evaluation infrastructure that keeps quality honest as models change underneath you. Expect to spend as much time designing evals as writing features — this is where the hard part lives.

Growth & GTM Engineering
** The pre-enrollment funnel: lead capture, counselor tooling, CRM integrations, and the NSET admissions platform. Here AI shows up as call-transcript intelligence, natural-language querying over internal data, and copilots that make a counselor's next conversation materially better.

What we're looking for
** Strong fundamentals in data structures, algorithms and problem solving — this still matters, and we will test it.
** Ability to build a non-trivial web application end to end, with real comfort in JavaScript and at least one modern frontend framework (React preferred).
** Working knowledge of at least one backend language and one relational database, including how to write a query that doesn't fall over at scale.
** A sense for both high-level system design and the low-level details underneath it.
** You care about writing clean, maintainable, well-tested code even when the problem is hard and the deadline is close.
** Evidence that you build things. Side projects, open-source contributions, hackathons, a repo you're proud of — we would rather see what you've shipped than what you've listed.
** Comfort working across functions, with product, design and mentors, and communicating clearly in writing.

Good to have
** You've built something real with LLM APIs — a RAG pipeline, an agent, a tool-using assistant— and can talk honestly about where it broke.
** Experience with an AI framework or orchestration library (LangChain, LlamaIndex, or your own thing you wrote because the frameworks annoyed you).
** An instinct for evaluation: how do you know your AI feature is actually good, and better than last week?
** Hands-on Ruby on Rails, or willingness to pick it up quickly.
** Experience with AWS and owning a piece of infrastructure.
** Product-building experience — you've had users, not just commits.

Tech stack
** Application: Ruby on Rails, Python on FastAPI, React, Node.js
** Data: MySQL, DynamoDB, Redis, Redshift
** Infrastructure: AWS (ECS and friends)
** AI: Frontier LLM APIs, vector stores, retrieval and agent frameworks, in-house evaluation tooling
** You will not know all of this on day one. Nobody does. We care that you learn fast.

What you get out of it
** Real ownership, early. You get a scoped problem and the room to solve it, not a queue of tickets. Your code reaches learners in days.
** Frontier work. You will build with the newest models and the newest patterns, at a company where AI is the product direction rather than a slide in a deck.
** Mentorship compounds. Our team includes engineers from Google, Meta, Amazon and high-growth startups, and the same people who teach thousands of engineers will be reviewing your pull requests.
** A front-row seat. A high-growth B2C and B2B business where you can see the line from what you built to what changed for a learner.
** A real path forward. Strong interns get pre-placement offers. Several of our full-time engineers started exactly here.
** Stipend and perks. Competitive stipend, free meals, and an office people actually want to be in.
