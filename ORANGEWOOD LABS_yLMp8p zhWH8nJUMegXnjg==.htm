Job Profile Details:
Designations: Mechanical Designer, Embedded Engineer, Founder's Office, AI Intern, Robotic Solutioning Engineer

Mechanical Design Intern:
** Design mechanical components, assemblies, fixtures, grippers, and robotic structures.
** Create 3D CAD models, assemblies, and engineering drawings.
** Work on robot mounts, end-effectors, conveyors, tooling, and automation mechanisms.
** Prepare BOMs and manufacturing drawings.
** Support prototyping, fabrication, assembly, and testing.
** Work with vendors for machining, fabrication, sheet metal, and 3D printing.
** Troubleshoot mechanical issues and iterate designs based on testing.
** Collaborate with robotics, embedded, AI, and solutioning teams.
** Support installation and deployment of robotic systems.

Requirements
** Pursuing or recently completed a degree/diploma in Mechanical Engineering or related field.
** Good understanding of mechanical design fundamentals.
** Knowledge of SolidWorks, Fusion 360, AutoCAD, or similar CAD software. ** Understanding of engineering drawings, tolerances, materials, and manufacturing processes.
** Strong interest in robotics and automation.
** Hands-on and problem-solving mindset.

Good to Have
** 3D printing and prototyping experience.
** Knowledge of robotics, actuators, motors, bearings, and mechanisms.
** Experience designing grippers or robotic fixtures.
** Basic knowledge of pneumatic systems.

What You Will Learn
** Industrial robotic system design.
** End-effector and workcell design.
** Rapid prototyping and manufacturing.
** Real-world robot deployment and automation.
** Working with cross-functional engineering teams.

Embedded Engineer Intern:
** Develop and test firmware for microcontrollers and embedded platforms.
** Interface sensors, motors, encoders, actuators, and other peripherals.
** Work with communication protocols such as CAN, RS485, UART, SPI, I2C, Ethernet, and Modbus.
** Debug hardware and firmware issues.
** Assist with PCB testing and hardware integration.
** Develop diagnostic and testing tools.
** Integrate embedded systems with robotic controllers.
** Work with robotics and software teams on system integration.
** Support prototype development and real-world testing.
** Document firmware, hardware interfaces, and test results.

Requirements
** Pursuing or recently completed Electronics, Electrical, Embedded Systems, or related engineering degree.
** Good understanding of electronics and embedded systems.
** Programming knowledge in C/C++.
** Familiarity with microcontrollers such as STM32, ESP32, Arduino, etc.
** Understanding of basic communication protocols.
** Strong debugging and problem-solving skills.

Good to Have
** Linux and Python knowledge.
** ROS/ROS2 exposure.
** CAN/CANopen or industrial communication experience.
** Motor control and encoder experience.
** Experience with oscilloscopes, multimeters, and logic analyzers.

What You Will Learn
** Embedded systems for robotics.
** Motor and actuator control.
** Industrial communication.
** Hardware-software integration.
** Testing and deployment of robotic systems.
